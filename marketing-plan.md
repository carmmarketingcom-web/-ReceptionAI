# Marketing Plan — ReceptionAI

## Positioning

**One-liner:** "An AI receptionist that answers calls, books appointments, and never sleeps — for the price of a dinner out."

**Who this is for:** Busy service business owners (HVAC, plumbing, dental, legal, med spa) who miss calls during jobs or after hours and lose revenue.

**Why they buy:** They're losing $500-2,000/mo in missed bookings. $99/mo is a no-brainer ROI.

---

## Phase 1: Foundation (Week 1-2)

### 1. Call your own phone number
Dial (727) 966-7556. Hear the demo. If it doesn't impress you, fix it before showing anyone else.

### 2. Fix HTTPS
Upgrade cto.new to Plus. Customers will NOT sign up on an HTTP site. Non-negotiable.

### 3. Record a 60-second demo video
Screen-record yourself: call the number → AI answers → book an appointment → get SMS confirmation. Post on the homepage.

### 4. Set up Google My Business
Free listing for "AI Receptionist Service" — local SEO for people searching.

---

## Phase 2: Direct Outreach (Week 2-4)

### Strategy: Pick up the phone
The best channel for this product IS the product. Call local businesses yourself:

**Script:**
> "Hi, I'm Carlos. Quick question — do you ever miss calls when you're with a customer or after hours? I built an AI receptionist that answers 24/7, books appointments, and costs $99/mo. Want me to show you how it works?"

**Target list (10/day):**
- HVAC contractors in Clearwater/Tampa
- Plumbers
- Electricians
- Dental offices
- Law firms (small/mid)

Track responses. If 1 in 10 converts, you have 5 paying customers in month 1.

### Bonus: Call them FROM your AI number
Call from (727) 966-7556. When they miss your call, the AI calls back and says "Sorry we missed you." It sells itself.

---

## Phase 3: Paid Ads (Week 3+)

### Google Ads ($10-20/day)
**Keywords:** "AI receptionist," "virtual receptionist," "24/7 phone answering," "missed call solution"

**Landing page:** receptionai.store (it's already built)

**Target:** Service business owners searching for solutions. Budget: $300-600/mo to start.

### Facebook/Instagram Ads ($10/day)
**Audience:** Small business owners, 30-55, USA

**Creative:** "You missed a call. That call was worth $200. Get an AI receptionist for $99/mo."

This targets the pain point directly.

---

## Phase 4: Partnerships (Month 2+)

### Business associations
- Chamber of Commerce (Clearwater, Tampa)
- Trade associations (HVAC, plumbing, dental)
- Offer exclusive member discount: 20% off

### Referral program
- Current customers refer another business → both get 1 month free
- Low cost, high trust — referrals convert at 50%+

---

## What NOT to do (yet)
- ❌ Don't build a blog — content marketing works but takes 6+ months
- ❌ Don't hire an agency — costs too much too early
- ❌ Don't run LinkedIn ads — wrong audience
- ❌ Don't overthink the website — it's already good enough
