# ReceptionAI Logo Concepts

Three distinct SVG logo concepts designed for a trades-focused AI receptionist brand.

## Requirements
- Professional, bold, serious
- Trustworthy feel for HVAC/plumbing/electrical audience
- No speech bubbles, sparkles, or app-like elements
- Geometric mark + wordmark lockup

---

## Concept 1: Shield + Phone (`concept-1-shield-mark.svg`)
**Direction:** Trust & Protection
- Navy shield mark with integrated phone receiver silhouette
- Gold/amber accent evokes quality and reliability
- "ReceptionAI" with gold "AI" emphasis
- Tagline: "YOUR 24/7 AI RECEPTIONIST"
- Colors: #1a2332 (navy) + #f0a030 (gold)

## Concept 2: Hexagon Monogram (`concept-2-hexagon-monogram.svg`)  
**Direction:** Industrial & Technical
- Hexagonal shape nods to bolts/fasteners in trades
- Bold "R" monogram inside
- ALL-CAPS wordmark with cyan accent
- Tagline: "INTELLIGENT CALL HANDLING"
- Colors: #0f172a (dark navy) + #06b6d4 (cyan)

## Concept 3: Interlocking R+A (`concept-3-interlocking-mark.svg`)
**Direction:** Modern & Architectural
- Two interlocking geometric blocks forming abstract R+A
- Slate + blue/indigo gradient
- Lowercase wordmark (modern, approachable)
- Accent underline bar
- Tagline: "ALWAYS ANSWERS. NEVER SLEEPS."
- Colors: #1e293b (slate) + #3b82f6 (blue) + #6366f1 (indigo)
